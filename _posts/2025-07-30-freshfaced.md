## How I solved 'Fresh Faced - Finding the Founder'

BelingCat challenge

![FreshFaced Challenge](../assets/images/freshfaced.png "FreshFaced Challenge")

Given a newspaper photo clipping you were meant to find a corresponding youtube interview regarding the founder of Belingcat: Eliot Higgins and provide the code at the end of the link.

1. I first translated the words in the newspaper clipping by using autodetect and found that they were in croatian.

2. Inserted that phrase into google led me to a site "https://www.vecernji.hr/vijesti/kako-sam-otkrio-da-hrvati-salju-oruzje-u-siriju-532428" . I then went astray from the next logical step.

3. I then tried basic catchhrases that might trigger something on google such as: "eliot higgings youtube interview 2013" and "eliot higgins croatian 2013"

4. Then I searched for the actual conflict "Croatia sending weappons to Syria". I ran some sites though the wayback machine finding myself to Eliot Higgin's blog he operated under the alias "Brown Moses"

5. Then I searched for the biggest newspapers in croatia getting a list of a few I ran them with the same keywords as before: "eliot higgins 2013"

6. This leads me to a google query result of the same original link I got to and right b=loe that was the youtube video where the thumbnail matched the picture in the challenge and the youtube video was uploaded by the same person who wrote the first article I read (the logical step from there).
